# Gradix quick start: students

## 1. Sign in
- Create an account (or sign in with Google) and click the link in the verification email.
- Choose your language at the top (English, اردو, العربية, فارسی, سنڌي).

## 2. Take an exam
- Your dashboard lists your exams. Choose the question language and read the rules.
- If the exam is monitored, the screen tells you exactly what is recorded (leaving the tab or window, leaving
  fullscreen, copy/paste attempts; never your camera or screen). Accept to start.
- One question at a time, each with its own timer. You cannot go back. You have one attempt.
- Tap an answer or press 1-6. Answers save by themselves, even if the connection drops.
- **Read aloud** reads the question when your device has a voice for that language.

## 3. Your results
- Right after you submit: your score, results for each objective and your weak areas.
- **Study plan**: what to re-read, with the exact passages.
- **Practice**: as many extra questions as you like. Practice never changes your score.
- **Download report**: a PDF in your language.

## 4. Live quizzes
- When your teacher shows a code, open **Join live session**, type the 6 digits (and a nickname if you are not
  signed in) and wait for the start.
- Answer before the timer ends; the correct answer appears when your teacher shows it.

Help: the **?** button at the top.
